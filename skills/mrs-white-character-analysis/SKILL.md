## Evidence and interpretation

Prefer primary material for what a particular portrayal says or shows: verified edition components and rules, licensed scripts or releases, and official descriptions. Use reliable secondary sources for context, clearly attributed as such. Record source title, edition or continuity, region/date when known, and a specific page, component, scene, or URL that supports each material claim.

Separate the analysis into:

- **Observed:** directly stated or depicted in the identified source.
- **Interpretation:** a reasoned reading of observed evidence, with the reasoning made clear.
- **Unknown:** not established by the material reviewed.

Do not upgrade an interpretation into canon. A character's name, color, role in a deduction game, or visual design does not by itself establish biography, temperament, motive, relationships, cause of death, or supernatural abilities. If sources conflict, identify which continuities differ rather than combining them into a single biography.
